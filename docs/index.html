What Songlark does

Songlark listens in five-second windows, starting a new one every two and a half seconds, and runs a bird-sound model entirely on your iPhone. Nothing you record is uploaded. It helps you identify birds by sound, and like any listener it can be wrong, so it tells you how sure it is.

  • A live sonogram

    The song scrolls across the screen as you listen. When a bird stands out, its name is pinned to the phrase it sang.

  • About 9,700 species, on device

    The model covers about 9,700 bird species worldwide and works in a forest with no coverage, on a plane or abroad.

  • Honest matches

    Every result is marked Strong match, Likely or Possible, so you know how much weight to give it.

  • Your region, not your location

    Pick a region and Songlark weighs which birds are likely there. Birds that rarely occur are marked Unusual here, never hidden. No location permission is asked for.

  • Keep the songs

    Save a session to play it back, see the whole recording as a sonogram, analyse it again later, or make a share card. Import sound files from Files Pro

  • Life list and year list

    Add the birds you're sure of. Songlark keeps the date and the song, with counts for this year and for life. Export as CSV Pro

  • Dawn Chorus Pro

    Leave your iPhone on a windowsill overnight. Songlark keeps listening with the screen locked until the time you choose, and you wake up to every bird that sang.

  • Names in your language

    Species names in 12 languages from the IOC World Bird List, alongside English and scientific names. Optional species photos from iNaturalist, with credits.

Private by design

Songlark has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Identification happens on your iPhone, and your recordings stay there unless you share or export a file yourself. Species photos, if you switch them on, are requested from iNaturalist using only the bird's scientific name. Read the full privacy policy.
