Bird-sound model: Perch 2.0

Songlark's bird-sound model is built from Perch 2.0 (“Bird Vocalization Classifier”) by Google Research, published at kaggle.com/models/google/bird-vocalization-classifier.

Changes made: Songlark converted the model to run on iPhone and uses only its bird classes (9,706 of them, about 9,700 species).

Species names: IOC World Bird List

Species names in 12 languages, and the ranges used for regions, come from the IOC World Bird List and its multilingual list:

Gill, F, D Donsker & P Rasmussen (eds). 2026. IOC World Bird List (v15.2). doi: 10.14344/IOC.ML.15.2. worldbirdnames.org

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0). Songlark reformats the names and range information for display and for its region setting.

Species photos: iNaturalist

Optional species photos are loaded on demand from iNaturalist. Songlark shows only photos that their photographers have released under one of these licences:

  • CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
  • CC BY (Attribution)
  • CC BY-SA (Attribution-ShareAlike)

Each photo is shown with its photographer's credit and licence on the species page. The photos belong to their photographers and are used under those licences; the licence version for a given photo is the one recorded on iNaturalist.

Example recording

The example recording included in Songlark is a mix of public-domain recordings from:

  • The US National Park Service Yellowstone sound library (public domain, as works of the US federal government).
  • Wikimedia Commons, public-domain files “Carolina_Wren.ogg”, “Northern_Cardinal.ogg” and “Blue_Jay.ogg”.

Thank you to the recordists who placed these sounds in the public domain.
