Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

In short: Songlark identifies birds on your iPhone. Your recordings stay on your device unless you share or export them yourself. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The only data collected is your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID, used solely to check whether you have Songlark Pro.

This policy explains what the Songlark app for iPhone does with information. Songlark is made by Levani Topchishvili, an individual developer (“I”, “me”). If you have questions, see Contact below.

Data collected

Songlark collects only the following, and only to make the app work:

  • Purchase history: whether you have bought Songlark Pro, which plan, and its status (for example active, renewed or expired).
  • User ID: an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, created by the purchase service RevenueCat so it can keep track of your purchases. It isn't your name, email address or Apple Account, and it isn't linked to your identity.

This data is used for app functionality only: to unlock Pro and to restore it on a new device. It is not linked to your identity, it is not used for advertising, and it is not used to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

How purchases work

All payments are made through Apple's App Store. I never see your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Songlark uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to check what you have bought. When the app checks or restores purchases, it sends RevenueCat the anonymous app user ID together with the App Store transaction information Apple provides. As with any internet request, RevenueCat also receives your device's IP address. RevenueCat processes this data on my behalf under its own privacy policy.

Audio and recordings

  • Songlark uses the microphone only while you're listening, or during a Dawn Chorus session you start. iPhone shows the microphone indicator whenever it's in use.
  • Sound is analysed by a bird-sound model that runs entirely on your iPhone. No audio is sent to me or to anyone else for identification.
  • Recordings you save are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded unless you choose to share or export a file yourself, for example through the share sheet. Where a shared file goes is up to you and the app or service you send it to.
  • Your life list, year list, settings and saved recordings are stored on your device. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, this data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms. Deleting Songlark deletes this data from the device.

Location

Songlark does not access your location and never asks for location permission. The region used to weigh which birds are likely is one you choose in the app, or an initial guess taken from your iPhone's region setting (the one in Settings › General › Language & Region). You can change it at any time.

Species photos from iNaturalist

Species photos are optional. When they're switched on and you view a species, Songlark asks iNaturalist for a photo of that species using only its scientific name (for example Thryothorus ludovicianus). No recordings, identifications, lists or other personal data are sent.

This is a normal HTTPS request, so iNaturalist can see your device's IP address, as with any website. iNaturalist handles that under its own privacy policy. To stop these requests, switch off Load species photos in Songlark's Settings.

What Songlark does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. If you have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide me with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics; that is controlled by you and Apple, not by Songlark.
  • No tracking, and no sale or sharing of personal data with data brokers.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location or other personal data on your device. When you import a sound file, Songlark receives only the file you pick.

This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, trackers, external fonts or scripts. GitHub, as the host, may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operation; see GitHub's privacy statement. If you open an issue on GitHub, what you post is public and handled under GitHub's terms.

Retention and your choices

Data on your device stays until you delete it or delete the app. Purchase records held by RevenueCat are kept for as long as needed to provide and restore your purchases and to meet legal and accounting obligations. Because the app user ID is anonymous, I usually can't link it to you; if you'd like your purchase records deleted, or have a request under data-protection law (such as GDPR or CCPA), contact me and I'll help as far as I'm able. Deleting purchase records may mean Pro can't be restored afterwards, although Apple's own record of your purchase is not affected.

Children

Songlark isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change means the app collects more data, the App Store privacy information will be updated too.
