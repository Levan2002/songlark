Bird song guides

Practical guides for learning birds by ear: how to identify birds by sound, how to read a sonogram, and how to keep a life list and year list. Each one explains the idea first, then shows how to do it in Songlark.

Get Songlark on the App Store

The guides

  • How to identify birds by sound: a beginner's guideSongs versus calls, what to listen for, and a step-by-step way to put a name to what you are hearing.
  • How to read a bird song spectrogram (sonogram)What the axes and colors mean, and what whistles, trills, buzzes and clicks look like on a sonogram.
  • How to start a bird life list (and a year list)What a life list and year list are, what to record for each bird, and how to decide what counts.

Songlark is free to download. Live identification, the life list and species pages are free; Songlark Pro adds Dawn Chorus overnight sessions, unlimited recordings, sound-file import and CSV export.
